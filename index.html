NOAA Space Weather Scales Reference

Kp / Storm Level Category Visual Aurora Reach Potential Impacts
Kp 0 - 3 Quiet / Normal High Latitudes (Aurora Borealis normal zone) None
Kp 5 G1 (Minor) Quebec, Northern US, Scotland Weak power grid fluctuations, minor satellite operations impact.
Kp 6 G2 (Moderate) New York, Idaho, Central UK Voltage alarms at high latitudes, HF radio propagation fading at higher latitudes.
Kp 7 G3 (Strong) Illinois, Oregon, Northern Germany Voltage correction required; satellite navigation (GPS) degraded intermittently.
Kp 8 G4 (Severe) Southern US, Northern France Widespread voltage control problems; possible transformer damage; HF radio blackout.
Kp 9 G5 (Extreme) Texas, Florida, Mediterranean Complete HF radio blackout; severe grid shutdown risk; pipeline current issues.
